PromptCAD
=========

PromptCAD is a distribution of FreeCAD bundled with the PromptCAD
natural-language CAD workbench. Copyright (c) 2026 Robb Sharma.


What this package contains
--------------------------

1. FreeCAD, redistributed VERBATIM and UNMODIFIED.

   Copyright (c) the FreeCAD contributors.
   Licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or
   later (LGPL-2.1-or-later).

   The full licence text is in legal\FreeCAD-LICENSE.html and the complete
   list of FreeCAD's own third-party dependencies - including OpenCASCADE,
   Qt, Coin3D, Python and others - is in
   legal\FreeCAD-ThirdPartyLibraries.html.

   PromptCAD does not patch, recompile or otherwise alter any FreeCAD
   binary. The PromptCAD name, icon and splash screen come from
   bin\branding.xml, a configuration file that FreeCAD itself reads and
   supports for exactly this purpose. Deleting that file returns the
   application to stock FreeCAD appearance.

2. The PromptCAD workbench (Mod\PromptCAD).

   Copyright (c) 2026 Robb Sharma. Licensed under the MIT License; see
   Mod\PromptCAD\LICENSE.MD. It is a Python addon that calls FreeCAD's
   public API, distributed alongside FreeCAD rather than linked into it.

3. llama.cpp (backend\llama-server.exe and its libraries).

   Copyright (c) 2023-2026 The ggml authors.
   Licensed under the MIT License.
   Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp

   Redistributed as an unmodified upstream release build. PromptCAD uses it to
   run local GGUF models so that no download is needed on first use.

4. PromptCAD.exe, the distribution layer (Mod\PromptCAD\promptcad\distro),
   branding artwork and installer.

   Copyright (c) 2026 Robb Sharma. MIT.

No model weights are included in this package. PromptCAD links to models
published by their respective authors, each under its own licence, and those
licences govern your use of them. See https://alphaintellabs.com/promptcad.


Your rights under the LGPL
--------------------------

Because FreeCAD is licensed under the LGPL, you are entitled to obtain its
complete source code and to replace the bundled FreeCAD and Qt libraries
with your own versions. See legal\SOURCE-OFFER.txt for how to get the
source. The libraries in this package are dynamically linked and can be
substituted in place.
